About MIM Patna

Management education and entrepreneurship

MIM Patna describes itself as the first private initiative towards pursuing excellence in management education and entrepreneurship. The institute aims to change the present management education scenario.

Its programmes span management, technology and professional studies, with a focus on academic excellence and industry-relevant education.

PGDM

Post Graduate Diploma in Management

A management programme focused on building business knowledge and practical skills for today's organisations.

  • Study marketing, finance, human resources and operations
  • Develop business analytics and decision-making skills
  • Learn through live projects and industry internships
  • Duration: 2 years

MCA

Master of Computer Applications

An advanced computing programme covering software development and modern information technologies.

  • Build foundations in programming, data structures and databases
  • Explore software engineering, web and cloud technologies
  • Learn data science and AI fundamentals
  • Duration: 2 years

DRCC · Bihar Student Credit Card Yojana

Financial support for higher education

The Bihar Student Credit Card (BSCC) Yojana is a Bihar government education-loan scheme for eligible students pursuing higher education. Students can learn about eligibility and application steps through their District Registration and Counselling Centre (DRCC).
