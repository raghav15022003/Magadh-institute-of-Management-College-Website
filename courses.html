About the programme

Business knowledge with practical learning

The PGDM programme covers management fundamentals and key business functions. Curriculum areas listed by MIM include marketing, finance, human resources, operations and business analytics, supported by project-based learning.

Curriculum highlights

  • Management fundamentals and business communication
  • Marketing, finance, human resources and operations
  • Business analytics and decision-making
  • Live projects and summer internship
  • Professional skills and placement preparation

Who can apply?

About the programme

Computing foundations for technology careers

The MCA programme develops knowledge across core computing areas. MIM's listed curriculum highlights programming, data structures, databases, software engineering, web and cloud technologies, and data and AI fundamentals.

Curriculum highlights

  • Programming, data structures and algorithms
  • Database systems and software engineering
  • Web, mobile and cloud development
  • Data science and AI fundamentals
  • Major project and industry training

Admission at a glance

Who can apply?

Applicants with a bachelor's degree and Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level. Confirm current criteria with MIM.

Programme structure

The programme is listed as 2 years across 4 semesters. Confirm the current academic structure with the institute.
